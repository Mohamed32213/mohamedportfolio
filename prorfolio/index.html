PROJECT 01 Call Center

Salla Call Center Dashboard

Power BI Dataset: 450 × 12

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze call center performance, monitor operational KPIs, and identify call volume patterns.

Primary KPIs Monitored
Total Calls Offered (3M) Service Level (92%) Total Calls Handled (3M) Abandon Rate (1.4%) ASA & Forecast vs Actual
Key Insight: Call volume tends to be higher at the beginning of the month.
Recommendation: Improve call-handling efficiency and prepare additional resources during periods of higher call volume.

PROJECT 02 Sales & Retail

Adidas Sales Dashboard

Power BI Dataset: 9,648 × 13

A sales analytics dashboard created to evaluate Adidas sales performance, identify changes in sales trends, and extract actionable business insights.

Primary KPIs Monitored
Total Sales (900M) Operating Profit (332M) Units Sold (2M) Operating Margin (36.9%) State & Retailer Analysis
Key Insight: The West region of the USA represents a major portion of overall sales ($270M).
Recommendation: Strengthen advertising and promotional activities during periods where sales performance is weaker, while maintaining strong presence in high-performing regions.
GitHub
PROJECT 03 Marketing & F&B

Belabn Sales & Marketing Dashboard

Power BI Excel Dataset: 7,000 × 31

A sales and marketing dashboard focused on understanding product demand, sales performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

Primary KPIs Monitored
Total Sales (7.26M) Net Sales (6.61M) Units Sold (42K) Total Orders (7K) Topping & Category Mix
Key Insights: Product sales proportions are relatively similar; sales remain stable throughout the month; the Traditional category has stronger demand (33.9%).
Recommendation: Focus marketing efforts on the Traditional category while continuing to monitor product-level performance and customer demand.
GitHub
PROJECT 04 E-commerce / Furniture

Misuo E-commerce Dashboard

Microsoft Excel Dataset: 81 × 13

An Excel-based e-commerce analysis designed to identify top-performing cities, customer preferences, and best-selling products.

Primary KPIs Monitored
Total Sales (873,275) Total Orders (80) AVG Selling Price (10,916) On-Time Delivery Rate Fabric & Platform Mix
Key Insights: Cairo and Giza generate highest sales; Black and Brown are most selected colors; HomeSmart is the best-selling product.
Recommendation: Expand marketing activities into other governorates to reduce dependence on Cairo and Giza and increase geographic sales coverage.
